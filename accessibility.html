Accessibility Statement

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Our aim

Luxe Beauty by V aims to make its website usable by people with different abilities, devices and ways of accessing information. We use the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA as a design target. This statement is not a certification of full conformance or a guarantee that every page, device or third-party service is accessible.

Features included in the website

The current design includes a skip-to-content link, semantic headings, text alternatives for informative photographs, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus styles, text labels for icon-only controls, language indicators, a manual photo carousel without automatic advancement and support for reduced-motion preferences. The navigation can be used by keyboard, and the English/Hawaiian control exposes its selected state to assistive technology. A written address and direct contact options accompany the optional map.

Assessment and known limitations

The website has received code-level checks for selected navigation, labels, language behavior and local links. It has not yet completed a comprehensive accessibility audit across screen readers, browsers, mobile devices, high zoom and all interaction states. The Hawaiian content is an editorial draft and requires fluent-language review. Photograph descriptions may not communicate every visual treatment detail; contact Vanessa for another explanation. Further accessibility improvements may be needed as testing and feedback identify barriers.

Third-party services

Online booking is provided through Square. Google Maps and Instagram also operate independently. Their interfaces, language settings and accessibility are controlled by those providers. If you cannot use an external service, contact Vanessa for assistance with service information or appointment arrangements. You do not need to load the Google map to read the salon address.

Request assistance or report a barrier

Please email vanessalum73@yahoo.com or call (808) 936-5969 if you encounter a problem. If possible, identify the page, what you were trying to do, the difficulty encountered, and your browser or assistive technology. Do not include medical history, payment information or other sensitive details in an accessibility report. Vanessa can discuss an alternative way to obtain information or arrange an appointment. We will review reports and work toward a practical response; no fixed response time is promised.

Physical access and ongoing work

This statement concerns the website. For questions about parking, steps, entrances or physical access at Mika Salon, contact Vanessa before your visit so the actual arrangements can be confirmed. We plan to revisit accessibility when website features change, including before online intake and consent forms are introduced.
