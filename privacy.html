Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Luxe Beauty by V handles information in connection with this informational website and inquiries sent through its contact links. Luxe Beauty by V is the beauty-services business of Vanessa Lum, based at Mika Salon in Kamuela, Hawaiʻi. This policy describes the website as it is currently configured; it is not a treatment consent form or a HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices.

Information you provide

If you contact Vanessa by email or phone, you may provide your name, contact details, appointment questions and other information you choose to share. The website does not currently contain an active contact form, client account, newsletter signup, payment form, intake questionnaire or electronic signature form. The client-forms section is a placeholder and does not accept submissions.

Booking and payments through Square

Booking buttons take you to Square, where you can select services and appointments. Information you submit there is handled through Square and the business’s booking account. Square’s own privacy terms describe its collection, processing and security practices. This website does not collect payment-card details. For privacy questions about your appointment information, contact Vanessa; for Square’s platform practices, consult Square’s privacy notice.

Technical information and local storage

The hosting service may process technical information such as IP address, requested pages, browser information, timestamps and security logs when delivering the website or protecting it from abuse. The site saves a language preference in your browser under the key luxe-language. That preference is used to remember English or Hawaiian; the site does not attach a name or contact information to it. You can remove the preference by clearing site data in your browser. Fonts and the photo gallery are served from the website’s own files.

Maps, Instagram and external links

The Google map loads only after you choose Load map. Loading it connects your browser to Google, which may receive technical information and use cookies or similar technology under its own policies. You can use the written salon address without loading the map. Instagram photographs displayed in the gallery are local copies, not an embedded live Instagram feed. Following a link to Instagram, Google Maps, Square or another website takes you to that provider’s service, where its own policies apply. We do not control those services.

Analytics and advertising

The current website does not include a site-installed analytics tracker, advertising pixel, session-recording tool or newsletter tracker. Its language storage is not used for advertising. We do not sell information collected through this website or share it for targeted advertising. Hosting and external services may process technical data as described above; their practices are separate from the site’s own code.

How information is used and disclosed

Information provided in an inquiry may be used to answer questions, discuss services, assist with appointments, maintain relevant business communications and meet applicable obligations. Information may be processed by providers supporting those activities, including hosting, email, telephone and booking services. Information may also be disclosed when legally required, to respond to valid legal process, to protect safety or rights, or as part of a business transfer subject to appropriate safeguards. We do not publish private correspondence as website content.

Sensitive information and future intake forms

Please do not send medical histories, medication lists, identification documents, payment-card details or other sensitive information through ordinary email, Instagram messages or this public website. Ask Vanessa how to provide information needed for a particular service. Online intake and consent forms are not active. Before those forms are introduced, this policy will be updated to describe the form provider, information collected, purposes, recipients and storage arrangements. No claim is made that this website or the planned intake workflow is HIPAA compliant.

Client photographs

The website displays selected photographs of Vanessa’s work. To ask about a photograph that depicts you, request a correction or raise a concern about its use, contact Vanessa. Permission to receive a treatment and permission to publish a photograph are separate matters. Removing a photograph from this website does not remove copies previously shared on another service or saved by others.

Retention and security

Inquiries and related business records are retained for the period reasonably needed for their purpose and applicable legal, tax, insurance or recordkeeping obligations. Language storage remains on your device until it is changed or cleared. Hosting and platform providers determine retention for some of their own records. No universal retention period is promised for all information. Access to information should be limited to people who need it for the relevant business purpose. No internet service, email system or storage method can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and requests

You may contact Vanessa to ask about information held in connection with this website, request a correction, ask for deletion where appropriate, or raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify the request and may retain information where necessary for applicable obligations, dispute resolution or legitimate business records. These requests do not create rights beyond those provided by applicable law. You can avoid loading the map, choose not to follow external links, and clear the language preference in your browser.

Children

This website is intended for general information about beauty services and is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13 through an online form. If you believe a child has provided personal information in connection with the website, contact Vanessa so the matter can be reviewed. Service eligibility and any guardian-consent requirements are addressed separately before an appointment.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the website, service providers or information practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
